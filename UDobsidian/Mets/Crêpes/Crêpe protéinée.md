# Crêpe protéinée
Crêpe protéinée:  ngredients:

- 30g [@myproteinus](https://www.instagram.com/myproteinus/) Impact Whey
- 60g oat flour/blended oats
- 50ml almond milk
- 50g Greek yogurt
- 2 eggs
- 80g banana
- Cinnamon
- Sweetener
- Vanilla extract

Directions:

- Mix all ingredients together. Fry on medium heat until golden on each side! Makes 4-5 pancakes depending on size.

CALORIES: 667 [macros: 72c 19f 52p] - can be split into two servings!
