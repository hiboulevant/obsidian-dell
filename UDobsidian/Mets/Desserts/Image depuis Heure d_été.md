⠀
⠀
Recipe:
1 cup oat flour
1/2 cup coconut milk (from a carton not a tin)
1/4 cup chocolate chips
1 cup dates
1 tsp vanilla

Blend all ingredients except the chocolate chips - stir them in at the end! Makes four servings 🧡
