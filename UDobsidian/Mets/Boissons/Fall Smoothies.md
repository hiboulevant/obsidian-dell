5 Tasty AF Fall Flavored Smoothies (Swipe to see what’s in each) ⁠⁠
⁠⁠
You know how much I love a good smoothie, and who doesn’t love classic fall flavors like pumpkin, apple pie, etc.? That’s why I wanted to share these 5 delicious fall flavored smoothie recipes with you! They are so easy to make, plus they taste amazing and will be sure to put you in the fall spirit. Details on each below:⁠⁠
⁠⁠
What’s in the Pumpkin Pie:⁠⁠
\-1 banana ⁠⁠
\-1 pitted date ⁠⁠
\-½ tsp pumpkin pie spice ⁠⁠
\-½ cup pumpkin puree ⁠⁠
\-⅓ cup Greek yogurt ⁠⁠+ 1 cup almond milk
\-1 tbsp almond butter ⁠⁠
\-Collagen ⁠⁠
⁠⁠
What’s in the Apple Pie:⁠⁠
\-1 cup oat milk ⁠⁠
\-1/3 cup Greek yogurt ⁠⁠
\-1 medium apple⁠⁠
\-½ a banana ⁠⁠
\-1 pitted date ⁠⁠
\-1 tbsp cashew butter⁠⁠
\-¼ tsp cinnamon + allspice + nutmeg ⁠⁠
\-½ tsp vanilla extract ⁠⁠
⁠⁠
What’s in the Cranberry Oatmeal:⁠⁠
\-1 cup oat milk ⁠⁠
\-½ cup cranberries ⁠⁠
\-½ cup oats ⁠⁠
\-1-2 beets ⁠⁠
\-¼ tsp cinnamon ⁠⁠
\-1 scoop vanilla protein powder ⁠⁠
⁠⁠
What’s in the Chocolate Salted Caramel:⁠⁠
\-1 cup oat milk ⁠⁠
\-1 banana ⁠⁠
\-2 dates ⁠⁠
\-½ cup cauliflower ⁠⁠
\-2 tbsp almond butter ⁠⁠
\-1 tbsp maple syrup ⁠⁠
\-¼ tsp salt ⁠⁠
\-chocolate protein powder⁠⁠
⁠⁠
What’s in the Sweet Potato Ginger:⁠⁠
\-1 cup almond milk⁠⁠
\-½ cup sweet potato puree ⁠⁠
\-⅓ cup Greek yogurt ⁠⁠
\-2tbsp almond butter ⁠⁠
¼ tsp ginger ⁠⁠
\-collagen ⁠⁠
⁠⁠
What to do:⁠⁠
1- Combine all ingredients in a high speed blender and blend until smooth.⁠⁠
2- Pour into your favorite glass, top with whipped cream if you like, and enjoy! ⁠⁠
